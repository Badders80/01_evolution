## Profile

I Stole A Manolo is a New Zealand-bred two-year-old filly by the Group 1 winning miler Satono Aladdin, trained from Matamata by Lance O'Sullivan and Andrew Scott. Coming from a deep New Zealand maternal line with a pedigree designed for speed and class, she is an exciting addition to the Wexford Stables roster.

A bay filly that possesses real presence and a strong, correct moving action. Born on August 30, 2023, she is a daughter of the sensational sire Satono Aladdin, who has taken the New Zealand breeding scene by storm with his first crops.

She is out of the Jimmy Choux mare Canuhandleajandal, a family noted for its toughness and performance. Currently in the early stages of her racing education at the world-class Matamata facilities, she is being carefully prepared to follow in the footsteps of Wexford's many Group 1 champions.

## Trainer — Lance O'Sullivan & Wexford Stables

Wexford Stables is a name synonymous with excellence in New Zealand racing history. Founded by Hall of Fame trainer Dave O'Sullivan, the legacy continues today under the leadership of Lance O'Sullivan ONZM and Andrew Scott.

Lance O'Sullivan is a true icon of the sport. Before transitioning to training, he was a 12-time Champion Jockey in New Zealand, recording 2,479 career wins, including a world-record victory in the 1989 Japan Cup aboard Horlicks.

Since taking the reins at Wexford, Lance has applied that same elite horsemanship to training, producing over 600 winners and multiple Group 1 champions, including:

- Waitak (Gr.1 Railway Stakes winner)
- Molly Bloom (NZ 1000 Guineas winner)
- Rocket Spade (NZ Derby winner)
