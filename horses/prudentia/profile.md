## Profile

Prudentia is a New Zealand-bred four-year-old mare by Proisir, trained from Matamata by Lance O'Sullivan and Andrew Scott at Wexford Stables. She has already recorded a maiden victory and performed across a range of distances and track conditions.

Her win came over 1400 metres at Tauranga, where she handled testing Heavy conditions to score decisively. Since breaking her maiden, she has stepped into Rating 65 Benchmark company, continuing her preparation against stronger opposition. Her race record reflects early progression through maiden grade and exposure to open competition within New Zealand's domestic programme.

## Trainer — Lance O'Sullivan & Wexford Stables

Wexford Stables is a name synonymous with excellence in New Zealand racing history. Founded by Hall of Fame trainer Dave O'Sullivan, the legacy continues today under the leadership of Lance O'Sullivan ONZM and Andrew Scott.

Lance O'Sullivan is a true icon of the sport. Before transitioning to training, he was a 12-time Champion Jockey in New Zealand, recording 2,479 career wins, including a world-record victory in the 1989 Japan Cup aboard Horlicks.

Since taking the reins at Wexford, Lance has applied that same elite horsemanship to training, producing over 600 winners and multiple Group 1 champions, including:

- Waitak (Gr.1 Railway Stakes winner)
- Molly Bloom (NZ 1000 Guineas winner)
- Rocket Spade (NZ Derby winner)

Operating out of the world-class facilities in Matamata, Wexford Stables maintains one of the best strike rates in the country.
