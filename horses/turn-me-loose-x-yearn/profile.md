## Profile

A 2YO bay filly by Group 1 sire Turn Me Loose out of the Group 2-winning mare Yearn, currently in early education with Stephen Gray at Copper Belt Lodge, Palmerston North.

This filly combines a proven Group 1 sire line with a multiple-stakes-performed dam. Turn Me Loose has already produced a Group 1 winner (Licketysplit) and 89 winners from his first crops. Yearn was a high-class race mare with 7 wins and nearly $340,000 in prizemoney, including the Group 2 Auckland Thoroughbred Breeders Stakes and placings in the Group 1 Thorndon Mile and Group 2 Rich Hill Mile.

Bred by C W Kwok and offered at the 2025 Karaka National Yearling Sales Series (Lot 413, account Windsor Park Stud) before being withdrawn — retained for racing rather than sale. The filly is registered on the NZTR Stud Book with full identification (microchip, brands, DNA typing).

## Trainer — Stephen Gray & Copper Belt Lodge

Stephen Gray is a Group 1-winning racehorse trainer with over three decades in the industry. After 24 years at the helm of one of Singapore's most successful racing stables, Gray returned to New Zealand and now trains from Copper Belt Lodge near Palmerston North — the private training centre he owns with his father, veteran horseman Kevin Gray.

Gray trained over 825 winners in Singapore, including six local Group 1 victories, two Singapore Derby wins, the Singapore Gold Cup, and back-to-back Lion City Cup wins with Lim's Cruiser. His horses have competed at Royal Ascot, in Hong Kong, and Dubai.
