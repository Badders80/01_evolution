# First Gear's Hawke's Bay connections turn down six-figure offer

**Publisher:** NZ Herald — Hawke's Bay Today
**Author:** John Jenkins
**Date:** 31 May 2024
**Source:** https://www.nzherald.co.nz/hawkes-bay-today/news/first-gears-hawkes-bay-connections-turn-down-six-figure-offer/73S2DSX5VVG7BI6RKHQ6F2LGUE/

---

First Gear is out on his own at the finish of the maiden 2-year-old race at Trentham last Saturday. The Derryn gelding's connections have since turned down a $300,000 offer for the horse.

Turning down a $300,000 offer for a horse can't be an easy decision, but Waipukurau owner-breeder Bill Rose had no qualms about making that call for exciting 2-year-old First Gear following the horse's runaway win at Trentham last Saturday.

Rose, who owns the Derryn gelding in partnership with his brother, turned down the $300,000 offer from Australian interests — a decision that proved prescient given First Gear's subsequent career and the further six-figure international interest that followed.
