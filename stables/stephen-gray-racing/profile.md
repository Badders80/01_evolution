## Profile

Stephen Gray Racing operates from Copper Belt Lodge, the private training centre owned by Stephen Gray and his father, veteran horseman Kevin Gray. Located near Palmerston North, it provides Stephen with top facilities as he rebuilds a boutique, high-performance stable in New Zealand after 24 years training in Singapore.

With access to quality facilities and a revived domestic racing scene, SGR is the base for Stephen's return to NZ racing — bringing international experience, deep horsemanship, and long-term vision back to where it all began.
