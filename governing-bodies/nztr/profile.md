## Profile

New Zealand Thoroughbred Racing is the governing body for thoroughbred racing in New Zealand. All four Evolution Stables horses race under NZTR jurisdiction. NZTR maintains the Stud Book (via loveracing.nz), issues trainer licenses, and regulates race meetings.
