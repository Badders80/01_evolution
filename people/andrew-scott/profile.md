## Profile

Andrew Scott co-leads Wexford Stables alongside Lance O'Sullivan. Together they carry forward the legacy established by Hall of Fame founder Dave O'Sullivan, maintaining one of the best strike rates in New Zealand racing.

The O'Sullivan–Scott partnership has produced multiple Group 1 winners including Waitak (Gr.1 Railway Stakes), Molly Bloom (NZ 1000 Guineas), and Rocket Spade (NZ Derby).
