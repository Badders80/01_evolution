## Profile

Lance O'Sullivan is a true icon of New Zealand racing. Before transitioning to training, he was a 12-time Champion Jockey in New Zealand, recording 2,479 career wins — including a world-record victory in the 1989 Japan Cup aboard Horlicks.

Since taking the reins at Wexford Stables, Lance has applied that same elite horsemanship to training, producing over 600 winners and multiple Group 1 champions, including:

- **Waitak** — Gr.1 Railway Stakes winner
- **Molly Bloom** — NZ 1000 Guineas winner
- **Rocket Space** — NZ Derby winner

Operating out of the world-class facilities in Matamata, Wexford Stables maintains one of the best strike rates in the country.

## Legacy

Wexford Stables was founded by Hall of Fame trainer Dave O'Sullivan. The legacy continues today under the leadership of Lance O'Sullivan ONZM and Andrew Scott.
