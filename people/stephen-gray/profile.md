## Profile

Stephen Gray is a Group 1-winning racehorse trainer with a formidable international track record and more than three decades in the industry. After spending 24 years at the helm of one of Singapore's most successful racing stables, Gray returned to his family's Copper Belt Lodge in New Zealand to continue his training career.

As the last of the original license holders at Singapore's iconic Kranji racecourse, Gray trained over 825 winners, including six local Group 1 victories. His career highlights include:

- **Two Singapore Derby wins** (2007, 2021)
- **Singapore Gold Cup** (2016)
- **Queen Elizabeth II Cup** (2021)
- **Back-to-back Lion City Cup wins** with Lim's Cruiser (2017, 2018)

His horses have competed on the world stage, with runners at Royal Ascot, in Hong Kong, and Dubai.

Gray's Singapore operation peaked with 60 horses in work, and his longevity in Asia's most competitive jurisdiction is a testament to both his training ability and business acumen.

## Return to New Zealand

Now based near Palmerston North, Stephen trains from Copper Belt Lodge, the private training centre he owns with his father, veteran horseman Kevin Gray. With access to top facilities and a revived domestic racing scene, Gray is committed to rebuilding a boutique, high-performance stable in New Zealand, bringing his international experience, deep horsemanship, and long-term vision back to where it all began.

## Dual Role

Stephen is both the trainer and an owner. He trains First Gear from Copper Belt Lodge and holds ownership interests in the horse.
