# Section 2 — Description of the Virtual Asset

A detailed description of the Virtual Asset that will be Issued, including but not limited to all features, uses or other characteristics.

The HLT Virtual Asset issued represents a tokenized lease agreement for a specific percentage stake in a racehorse, granting the holder access to the horse's "Earnings" for a fixed period.

This structure enables a low-risk, flexible entry into racehorse industry participation and contribution to the equine industry while maintaining compliance with industry regulations. The Virtual Asset does not confer legal title in the horse(s) to tokenholders or any guarantee of income, profits or returns. Furthermore, the HLT does not purport to have any stable or pegged asset value or confer any claim over the relevant horse; the reference asset is the lease agreement.

## 2.1 Key Features & Characteristics

### 2.1.1 Fixed-Term Lease Model

Tokenholders obtain a non-exclusive digital slot to participate in the operational lease cycle of a racehorse (e.g., 1% or more) for a predetermined period of not less than 12 months.

The lease duration is a fixed term set by the racehorse owner, with such particulars for each horse set out in the relevant virtual asset description on the Tokinvest platform. Leases are structured to reflect a defined period of the horse's Earnings — whether in training, racing, or at stud.

Given the cyclical nature of the industry, owners retain flexibility to structure leases according to their goals. However, all lease terms are counted in full calendar months, with a minimum duration of 12 months hardwired into the HLT product structure.

### 2.1.2 Virtual Asset Reward Structure

Tokenholders receive access to a non-guaranteed performance-based reward structure as recognition for supporting the care and promotion of the horse during the lease period.

Tokenholders are eligible to receive a variable reward, set by the horse owner, as recognition for participating in the operational journey of the horse.

This range gives owners flexibility to adjust based on the horse's quality and commercial potential — retaining more upside in elite horses while still ensuring meaningful rewards for tokenholders.

#### 2.1.2.1 Potential Revenue Streams

Potential Revenue Streams (if any) that will contribute to Rewards include:

- **Race Winnings (Prize Money):** Standard earnings from official race results, including base prize pools and bonus schemes.
- **Sponsorship & Endorsements:** Commercial deals involving the horse, silks, syndicate, or related promotions.
- **Media & Naming Rights:** Earnings from appearances, interviews, documentaries, or naming rights.
- **Digital Asset Income:** Proceeds from the sale or resale of digital memorabilia (e.g. NFTs), including royalties from secondary market transactions.
- **Breeding or Exit Proceeds:** If the horse is sold or earns breeding-related revenue during the lease, tokenholders may receive a share of those proceeds (subject to the standard discretionary buyout clause: 3x the remaining lease value).
- **Insurance Proceeds:** Where applicable, any insurance claim paid out during the lease term.
- **Appearance Fees:** Income from events, parades, or promotional appearances.
- **Merchandising & Hospitality:** Revenue from branded merchandise or race-day experiences for tokenholders.
- **Data Licensing:** Earnings from licensing the horse's biometric or performance data.
- **Other Commercial Revenue:** Any other reasonable and verifiable income directly attributable to the horse during the lease period.

### 2.1.3 Utility-Based Benefits

Tokenholders may also be entitled to additional utility benefits to be defined by the relevant horse owner. These may include:

- Access to behind-the-scenes updates or training data
- Token-gated live streams or trainer Q&As
- Invitations to race day events or digital experiences
- Exclusive rights to purchase related NFTs or memorabilia

### 2.1.4 No Additional Financial Responsibilities

Tokenholder subscription funds will be used for the care, maintenance, training and upkeep of the specified horse(s).

Other than the subscription, tokenholders do not bear any additional financial responsibility for training, care, or veterinary costs.

Leasing is purely an exchange of cashflows (if any) based on race performance or other earnings as outlined above, making this VA a clearly defined reward structure but with no guarantee of any income. Tokenholder returns are a predetermined and specific share of all earnings achieved by the individual horse(s) over the life of the token, received in proportion to their fractional investment in the care, maintenance, training and support for the horse.

### 2.1.5 Regulated & Transparent Process

#### 2.1.5.1 NZTR Registration & Oversight

The HLT Hottathanafantasy NZ01 lease is registered with NZTR, ensuring full regulatory compliance.

Horses are managed under an NZTR-registered Authorised Syndicator or Lease Manager providing oversight and governance.

Any change to the horse's status — including spelling, injury, suspension or deregistration — is communicated to the Lease Manager, who advises of an update to the token status on the Tokinvest investor platform, ensuring strict alignment with NZ records.

NZTR's governance processes, Code of Conduct, and oversight of ownership and registration activity provide a clear layer of independent scrutiny. A lease can only be cancelled if the horse is officially deregistered with NZTR (Form SR24 submitted and approved). This protects investors against any scenarios where an unscrupulous owner may try and game the lease process for short-term gain (such as pump and dump).

If investors are dissatisfied with any action taken by the Lease Manager, the owners, or Racing Manager, there is formal recourse through NZTR's independent dispute and integrity processes.

#### 2.1.5.2 AML/KYC & Platform Security

Transactions require AML/KYC verification on the Tokinvest Investor platform.

Tokens can only be purchased by whitelisted and pre-approved users via the Tokinvest platform. Once purchased, tokens are custodied in segregated wallets on Hex Trust and managed on behalf of tokenholders by Tokinvest.

All tokens are issued and burnt by Tokinvest and transfer of ownership takes place strictly via its platform to ensure that only pre-approved whitelisted participants can transact in the tokens.

#### 2.1.5.3 Issuance Mechanics

There are no pre-defined phases to the offering of the HLT as there might be with a non-real-world asset token. Each issuance relates to a new and specific lease which in turn represents a new and specific share of the rewards related to an individual racehorse asset. See Section 5.5 below for details around potential subsequent issues.

There are no pre-sales or discounted offers for early purchasers. The tokens are listed on the Tokinvest platform at the issue price until the full value of the lease is sold or the remaining amount of the lease is withdrawn.

Tokens minted are limited to the pre-determined amount of the lease. Once all tokens related to a lease are sold there is no further offering against that lease and it cannot be oversold.

There is no subscription process or period for the HLT at this time. Tokens are offered for sale on the platform at a fixed price until tokens are sold or the horse owner delists the offering and removes any unsold tokens. Offerings do not need to sell out to be successful.

#### 2.1.5.4 Fund Handling & DVP Process

All investor funds are held securely by Tokinvest in their CMA with Zand Bank in the UAE. No transfer of funds takes place until the tokens have been minted and no tokens are minted until the full amount of investor funds have been received. This allows for a DVP process which protects investor funds and tokens both.

Whitelisted investors can deposit AED funds to their account with Tokinvest DMCC. Upon receipt of funds, tokens can be purchased and the requisite amount will be debited from their account accordingly. All token flows from the issuer to the investor are credited to their account with Tokinvest upon receipt where they can be withdrawn when desired.

In the unlikely circumstance that a lease offer is withdrawn prior to issuance for commercial or compliance reasons, Tokinvest will not mint tokens for this lease.

Once a lease is officially listed on the platform and made available for sale, the lease can only be terminated if the horse is no longer fit to race or becomes otherwise ineligible (see Section 7 — Lease Termination and Modification of Rights below). The lease issuer does not have the right to cancel the offering once tokens have been issued to investors.

#### 2.1.5.5 Custody & Token Recovery

Tokinvest uses Hex Trust as their VARA licensed custodian for digital assets. Upon successful purchase of tokens on the Tokinvest DMCC platform, the operations team will transfer the investor tokens to their individual wallet with Hex Trust within 48 hours. Tokinvest maintain the CAP table of all assets and can thus restore any lost or stolen tokens to the rightful investor should a negative event occur.

### 2.1.6 Secondary Market

Where sufficient investor interest permits, Tokinvest will offer existing tokenholders the opportunity to list their tokens for sale on the platform before lease expiry.

There is no accrual calculation or rewards split when tokens are transferred between payment periods — quarterly payment is due solely to the owner of the token on the payment date. This is the same model as employed in financial markets for equities which pay a dividend to the registered shareholder on the dividend date.

When a tokenholder sells their token on the secondary market they immediately forgo any future payments from the token, all of which pass to the buyer.

It is the responsibility of both buyer and seller to check the performance of the horse in the current period to determine any payments already due to the token at the time of transaction. Race history and horse performance is detailed on the Race Record tab of the offering page for each specific horse on the investor platform. Payment dates are detailed on the Timeline tab.

Buyers and sellers will receive an on-screen message reminding them of their responsibilities when they enter an order on the secondary market page on the Tokinvest platform.

### 2.1.7 Simple Exit Mechanism

At the end of the predetermined and non-negotiable fixed term of the lease, the tokenholder's entitlement to earnings ceases. At this time the token will be burnt. There is no redemption value associated with the token itself at expiry; payments to tokenholders are based solely on the rewards detailed above and accrued during the life of the token.

### 2.1.8 Future Plans

"HLT – Hottathanafantasy NZ01" is the third tokenised horse lease asset tokenised globally and is attracting high interest from the international racing community. The first milestone will be the issuance and sale of the tokens successfully.

It is envisaged that it will be the second of a number of issuances due to the broad interest from industry participants globally, with leases being tokenised under the same structure and using the same token profile (with a unique name for each individual lease to ensure ease of identification and non-fungibility).

Resources have been allocated for onboarding of new investors, marketing and the cost of minting, managing and burning the tokens.

### 2.1.9 Target Market

Horse ownership has traditionally been limited to high-net-worth individuals who have been able to purchase a stake in a syndicate. As a global sport, it has an enormous following from Royal Families to the everyday person. By offering this token to the public, an opportunity is created for everybody to enter the realms of having an economic interest in a horse.

The token is targeted at non-US investors of all categories, who have an interest in supporting the future development of the equine industry. The target audience includes, but is not limited to: high-net-worth individuals, racing syndicates and clubs, and international racing fans looking to enjoy events outside of their own domestic calendar.

Investor categories eligible to invest in the HLT token are Institutional, Qualified and Retail. All investor categories have equal rights as tokenholders.

Investors must be over the age of 18 to purchase HLT tokens. There is no upper age limit as horse racing is a sport enjoyed by all ages.

### 2.1.10 Trading Platform

After issuance the token will only be available for purchase on the Tokinvest DMCC investor platform. Tokinvest will also support secondary market trading of the tokens should there be sufficient investor interest.

## 3.1 Rights of Lease Holders

### 3.1.1 Race Winnings Participation

Lease holders receive 75% of winnings generated by their leased stake.

Payouts are distributed quarterly on a paid-once-paid basis via the Tokinvest platform directly to the tokenholders.

### 3.1.2 Fixed-Term Financial Exposure

Tokenholders are only financially exposed for the lease duration and for a known and pre-specified amount, limiting long-term risk.

No obligation to renew after the term expires.

### 3.1.3 Market Access & Flexibility

Tokenholders can lease multiple horses to diversify exposure.

**Buyout clause** — if a horse is sold during the lease period, the owners will honour the remaining duration of the lease with a buyout at 3x the remaining lease value. For example: if a token holder had acquired a 1000 AED fractional HLT for a 12-month period, and that horse was sold with 6 months remaining on the HLT, then the token holder will be entitled to a payout of 3 × 6/12 × 1000 = 1500 AED.

### 3.1.4 No Rights of Ownership of the Underlying Asset

Each Horse Lease Token represents a fractional beneficial interest in a time-bound lease of a registered racehorse. It does not confer legal ownership of the horse in part or in full. The lease is structured and registered in accordance with the relevant Rules of Racing and is proportionate to the percentage issued (e.g. 1% token = 1% leased interest). Fractionalisation is achieved through tokenisation of the lease percentage and not the ownership of the underlying asset.

See also Section 7 below — Lease Termination and Modification of Rights — for details of situations where these rights may be modified and token status changed.

# Section 5 — Issuance Structure

A detailed description of the issuance structure of the Virtual Asset, in particular the number of Virtual Assets that will be issued, the issuance schedule, when all the Virtual Assets will be made available and how many will be allocated or retained by the Issuer.

The Virtual Asset represents a fixed-term lease agreement, granting holders proportional entitlement to race winnings and other earnings (if any) for the HLT duration. The issuance structure is designed for efficiency, transparency, and minimal complexity, aligning with industry best practices and permitting tokenholders to contribute to the further growth of the equine industry.

## 5.1 Lease Token Issuance Reward Model

The horse owner determines the lease size (e.g., offering 10% of the horse), lease duration (always in full months, minimum of 12 months), and listing price per percentage (e.g., $X per 1%).

There is no minimum target subscription threshold for a listing and so at no point will an investor have made a purchase for the offer to be cancelled. Unsold lease stakes remain with the owner and can be re-listed in future cycles. Investor accounts with Tokinvest are only debited when the tokens are purchased and any failed purchase will see the funds immediately returned to their account.

## 5.2 Minting & Token Supply

**Mint on Purchase** — Tokens are only ever minted when purchased by an investor on a transaction-by-transaction basis. This avoids unnecessary supply and the risk of unsold tokens being left in the issuer wallet. As detailed in Section 2 above.

Unsold lease stakes remain listed on the platform until such times as the owner wishes to withdraw and made available for future leasing. This ensures only active tokenized leases exist in circulation.

## 5.3 Allocation & Retention of Virtual Assets

Investors receive tokens upon purchase.

Unsold stakes remain with the owner and are not tokenized unless purchased.

Tokinvest does not retain ownership of any of these Virtual Assets.

## 5.5 Subsequent Offers

The HLT is a token standard that is expected to be used for future issuances. Each tokenised lease is a standalone product tied to a defined time period and percentage of a specific horse. Different racehorses will have their leases tokenised and existing horses may have lease renewals tokenised or owners may wish to increase the tokenised proportion of a racehorse with an existing lease.

There is no expectation that any of these offers will dilute the value of tokens already issued as they represent different and exclusive shares of the rewards available, as:

- Tokenising the lease of a new racehorse should not impact the value of tokens outstanding for another racehorse as they represent different assets with their own potential reward stream.
- A further lease on the same horse which has already been tokenised in part should not impact the value of tokens outstanding as these tokens will have their own proportional share of the overall rewards and will not reduce the entitlements for existing tokens in any way. Each issuance is separate with distinct terms.

## 5.6 Tokenholder Protections

The New Zealand racing industry is one of the most highly regulated in the world. Every aspect of syndication is governed by NZTR, with independent oversight from the Racing Integrity Board. From licensed trainers and regular veterinary inspections to compulsory lease registration and dispute resolution, the system ensures the horse's welfare and protects investors' interests at every stage. These real-world regulatory protections ensure that the underlying asset (the horse) cannot be unilaterally withdrawn, mistreated, or mismanaged without significant legal, financial, and reputational consequences — giving investors a strong layer of protection that is rare in digital asset offerings.

### 5.6.1 Licensed Handlers and Professional Management

- **Authorised Syndicator:** All syndicates must be registered and authorised by NZTR, which requires meeting high standards for compliance, transparency, and investor protection.
- **Licensed Trainer:** Only trainers licensed by NZTR can manage the care and training of the horse. They must adhere to strict codes of conduct, animal welfare standards, and are subject to regular audits.
- **Qualified Stable Staff and Work Riders:** Every person who handles, rides, or cares for the horse must hold a current NZTR licence, ensuring all hands-on management is performed by vetted professionals.
- **Routine Veterinary Inspections:** Regular health and welfare checks by qualified veterinarians are mandatory. Any issues or injuries are reported to NZTR, providing ongoing assurance of the asset's wellbeing.

### 5.6.2 Regulatory Oversight and Enforcement

- **New Zealand Thoroughbred Racing (NZTR):** NZTR is the central regulatory authority responsible for registering syndicates, trainers, leases, and all official agreements. This ensures that all activities and changes are monitored and controlled.
- **Compulsory Lease and Syndicate Registration:** Every syndicate and lease agreement must be formally registered with NZTR, setting out detailed terms for ownership, management, and dispute resolution. Unilateral withdrawal or changes require NZTR approval.
- **Racing Integrity Board:** An independent body overseeing all participants, ensuring strict compliance with industry regulations. The Board has the authority to investigate, sanction, suspend, or disqualify individuals or syndicates for any breach.

### 5.6.3 Transparency, Reporting & Dispute Resolution

- **Regular Reporting to Investors:** Syndicate managers are obligated to provide frequent and transparent updates to all investors, including financial statements, race plans, and welfare updates for the horse.
- **Dispute Resolution Process:** NZTR offers a formal, independent process for resolving disputes, protecting investor rights in the event of any breach of agreement or concern over the management of the asset.

Investor protections relating to the lease are detailed in the risk disclosure.

While NZTR generally does not involve itself directly in private contractual disputes between parties, it maintains authority over lease registrations, horse eligibility, and racing participation. Failure to adhere to NZTR's stipulated requirements (such as properly registering leases or complying with lease conditions) can lead to disciplinary actions — including fines, suspension, or de-registration of horses from racing events — which would halt token trading and invalidate related lease obligations.

NZTR is supportive of digital syndication initiatives, as increasing ownership and participation aligns directly with their core objectives. Although they cannot directly participate — as this falls outside their regulatory mandate — they remain supportive provided these initiatives fully adhere to existing regulations and procedures.

The NZTR Rules of Racing are linked for reference — [Rules of Racing](#).

If a leased horse is injured, lease tokens may be temporarily suspended until further notice. This means they will not be listed for sale on the Tokinvest platform until the horse has returned to race fitness.

Any changes to the horse's eligibility (such as withdrawal from racing due to injury or investigation) must be reported to NZTR and may impact the lease agreement. ## 7.3 Lease Termination and Modification of Rights

A lease will be deemed terminated when the registered owner or manager submits, and NZTR (or the relevant racing authority) accepts, Form SR24, formally de-registering the horse from racing. At this point, all associated tokens will be burnt, and any outstanding returns distributed pro rata.

If a horse is re-registered within the original lease period, the original leaseholder will be offered the opportunity to resume their lease on the same terms and allocation before any new lease may be offered to others.

In the case of temporary setbacks (e.g. injury, spelling, or suspension), the lease remains in force. However, if costs materially reduce (such as when a horse moves from training to paddock), the owner is encouraged — though not obligated — to offer a refund, credit, or lease extension in good faith.

## 7.4 Token Classification System

A token classification system will be displayed on-platform to reflect the horse's status:

| Status | Label | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| 🟢 Green | Active | Horse is in full work and eligible to race or trial |
| 🟠 Orange | Temporarily Inactive | Horse is spelled, recovering from injury, or otherwise out of training |
| 🔴 Red | Under Investigation | Horse is suspended or under investigation by a racing authority |
| ⚫ Black | De-registered | Form SR24 submitted; horse no longer a registered racehorse |

### 7.4.1 Green and Orange Status

Green and Orange status are considered part of the normal racing lifecycle. During extended Orange periods, owners or the Racing Manager are encouraged to apply a lease cost reduction to reflect lower operating costs; however, this is not contractually enforceable and remains at their discretion.

### 7.4.2 Red Status

Red status reflects significant regulatory events; however, refunds or financial remedies are not automatically applied during Red status. If liability is determined by the relevant authority and the situation results in deregistration, investors may be eligible for refunds under Black status provisions.

### 7.4.3 Black Status

Black status represents a clear termination point with a structured refund process. Tokens are cancelled and burnt, and the lease is terminated. Investors receive a refund of any prepaid lease amount within 30 calendar days of deregistration being confirmed by NZTR and communicated to the Lease Manager. Refunds are paid by the Lease Manager to the Tokinvest platform and redistributed to investors' accounts immediately upon sight of funds or within 24 hours of their arrival in the Tokinvest CMA.

## 7.5 Re-Offer of Tokens on Re-Registration

If a lease is terminated and re-registered at a future date, affected tokenholders are offered the opportunity to resume their lease position on equivalent terms via an email to their registered address on the Tokinvest platform. These investors have 48 hours to respond to the email and express their interest before the offering becomes public.

Participation for prior tokenholders is optional and without financial penalty; however, there is no discount, and payment must be made as per the normal process to buy tokens. Only after the first right of refusal process is complete can lease rights be offered to new investors.

The deregistration process requires all outstanding tokens for the deregistered lease to be burnt. Therefore, if a lease is re-registered and offered for sale then new tokens will be minted and the offering listed on the Tokinvest platform.
