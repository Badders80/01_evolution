## Voice Principles

### 1. Institutional, Not Casual
We speak with the authority and precision of a financial institution. Thoroughbred investment is serious business. We never use slang, emojis in body copy, or overly casual language.

**Do:** "Every horse is identified by its NZTR-verified microchip."
**Don't:** "We track your horse with a microchip! 🐎"

### 2. Transparent, Not Vague
We explain what we do, how we do it, and why. If there's a limitation, we state it. If there's a risk, we disclose it. Trust is built through clarity.

**Do:** "HLT status transitions from draft to reviewed. Only an admin can move it to publish_ready."
**Don't:** "Your HLT will be processed soon."

### 3. Precise, Not Wordy
Every word earns its place. We prefer specific terms over general ones. We use the exact term (microchip, HLT, PDS) rather than a vague approximation (ID, document, form).

**Do:** "Microchip (15 digits)"
**Don't:** "Horse ID number"

### 4. New Zealand, Not Generic
We are a New Zealand business serving New Zealand investors. We use NZ English spelling, reference NZ racing bodies (NZTR, loveracing.nz), and acknowledge the NZ regulatory context.

**Do:** "Every NZ thoroughbred has a Stud Book page on loveracing.nz"
**Don't:** "You can look up your horse online"

## Terminology

| Term | Definition | Never Use |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Microchip | 15-digit NZTR-verified identifier, primary key for horses | "Horse ID", "chip number" |
| HLT | Horse Lease Terms — the legal structure for investment | "Lease", "contract", "agreement" |
| Term Sheet | Summary investment terms within an HLT | "Summary", "terms" |
| PDS | Product Disclosure Statement — NZ regulatory requirement | "Disclosure", "info doc" |
| SA | Syndicate Agreement — the binding ownership agreement | "Syndicate doc", "ownership form" |
| loveracing.nz | NZTR's Stud Book — the rosetta stone for horse data | "the racing site", "NZTR website" |
| Life Number | NZTR-assigned life number (e.g., NZ00427416) | "Racing number", "NZTR number" |
| Brands | Shoulder brands identifying a horse | "Markings", "tattoos" |

## Tone by Context

### Marketing Pages (Home, About)
- Warm but authoritative
- "Thoroughbred investment, reimagined."
- Focus on what's different: microchip-verified, institutional-grade, NZ-built

### Admin Interface
- Direct and functional
- Labels, not descriptions
- "Register Horse" not "Add Your New Thoroughbred Friend!"

### Error Messages
- Specific and actionable
- "Microchip must be exactly 15 digits" not "Invalid input"
- "Owner cannot be deleted while HLTs reference them" not "Delete failed"

### Legal Documents (Term Sheet, PDS, SA)
- Formal NZ legal language
- Full terms, no abbreviations on first use
- Compliance-first, marketing-second

## Writing Rules

1. **Oxford comma:** "horses, owners, and trainers"
2. **NZ spelling:** "organisation" not "organization", "customise" not "customize"
3. **No exclamation marks** in body copy (acceptable in success messages)
4. **Sentence case** for headings and buttons: "Register horse" not "Register Horse"
5. **Active voice:** "The API validates the microchip" not "The microchip is validated"
6. **Present tense** for descriptions: "Every horse has" not "Every horse will have"
